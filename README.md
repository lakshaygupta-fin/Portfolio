# Finance & Market Research Projects

This repository contains selected finance projects developed as part of my personal learning and market research.

## Projects

### 1. DCF Valuation Model

* Built a Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) model to estimate the intrinsic value of a company.
* Includes revenue projections, operating assumptions, free cash flow estimation, WACC, terminal value and sensitivity analysis.
* Developed in Excel for fundamental valuation and financial analysis.

**File:** `NATCO_DCF_Model.xlsx`

### 2. Trading Strategies & Technical Indicators

* Developed and backtested rule-based trading strategies using Pine Script.
* Utilized technical indicators including **MACD, RSI, EMA and VWAP**.
* Incorporated systematic entry, exit, stop-loss and target conditions for historical market analysis.

**File:** `Trading_Strategies_Indicators.docx`

## Disclaimer

These projects are intended for educational and personal research purposes only and do not constitute investment advice.
